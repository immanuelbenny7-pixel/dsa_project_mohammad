1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & GAME CONCEPT
================================================================================
"Pump Shift: Fuel Station Simulator" is a 3D browser-based simulation game that
places the player into the boots of a fuel station attendant working an active
shift. Operating under time pressure and customer expectations, the player must:
  - Direct and service incoming vehicles in strict FIFO order.
  - Select the correct fuel type (Regular 87, Premium 93, Diesel Bio).
  - Walk up to the vehicle, connect the fuel dispenser nozzle.
  - Aim the nozzle crosshair directly at the car's fuel cap target ring.
  - Pump the exact requested volume of fuel.
  - Collect payments and issue digital receipts.
  - Manage inventory, stock levels, customer satisfaction, and mistakes.
  - Use a LIFO transaction stack to reverse mistakes if an erroneous charge occurs.

The project demonstrates the practical application of fundamental computer 
science Data Structures (Queues, Stacks, Hash Maps, and Dynamic Arrays) 
seamlessly integrated into modern 3D graphics (Three.js WebGL) and responsive
gameplay mechanics.

2. GAME ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM DESIGN
================================================================================
The application is constructed purely using client-side web technologies with
zero third-party framework overhead, enabling maximum performance, portability,
and native browser compatibility.

Architectural Modules:
----------------------
1. Presentation Layer (index.html, style.css):
   - HUD Overlay: Top status bar, compact FIFO queue sidebar, minimized active
     dispenser card, interactive crosshair reticle, context prompts, toast alerts.
   - Modals: DSA Terminal inspector, Controls guide, End-of-shift report card.
   - Glassmorphic styling with CSS custom properties, backdrop filters, and 
     subtle micro-animations.

2. 3D Graphics Engine (js/models.js, three.min.js):
   - Three.js r128 WebGL renderer with PCF soft shadow maps and exponential fog.
   - Procedural Low-Poly 3D Assets:
     * Station canopy, asphalt ground, service bay yellow markings, shop building.
     * Two fuel pump islands with LCD screens, holsters, and hoses.
     * 5 Vehicle types (Sedan, SUV, Sports Car, Hatchback, Truck) with 8 distinct
       color palettes, turning wheels, headlights, and animated fuel caps.
     * Animated low-poly Attendant character with walk cycle and high-vis vest.
     * First-person viewmodel (animated right arm, safety glove, and fuel nozzle).

3. Data Structures Layer (js/dsa.js):
   - Standalone, cleanly decoupled implementations of Queue, Stack, Hash Map,
     and Dynamic Array with full invariant enforcement and event callbacks.

4. Audio Synthesis Engine (js/sound.js):
   - Web Audio API procedural sound synthesizer (synthesizes pumps, engines, 
     horns, cash register chimes, and UI feedback without external audio files).

5. Main Controller & Game Loop (js/game.js):
   - Shift clock, requestAnimationFrame loop, delta time calculations.
   - Input polling (WASD movement, mouse-look pointer lock, keyboard shortcuts).
   - Raycasting hit-detection for 3D interactions and fuel cap aiming.
   - Vehicle physics (acceleration, deceleration, queuing positions, departure).

3. CORE DATA STRUCTURES & ALGORITHM ANALYSIS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 FIFO QUEUE: VEHICLE TRAFFIC & ORDER PROCESSING (CustomerQueue)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Models the physical car service lane where vehicles arrive sequentially and
  must be serviced strictly in First-In, First-Out (FIFO) order.

Real-World Analogy:
  A single-lane gas station driveway where cars cannot jump over each other.

Implementation Details:
  - Class: `CustomerQueue`
  - Internal Structure: Array of car records `[car0, car1, car2, ...]`.
  - Operations:
    * `enqueue(car)`: Adds an arriving vehicle to the tail (Slot 3 -> Slot 0).
    * `dequeue()`: Removes and returns the serviced car at the head (Slot 0).
    * `peek()`: Inspects the vehicle currently at the pump without removing it.
    * `getPosition(carId)`: Returns the 1-based index (1 = at pump, 2 = 2nd in line).
    * `removeCar(carId)`: O(n) removal triggered when an impatient customer walks out.

Complexity:
  - Enqueue: O(1) amortized
  - Dequeue: O(n) for shift / O(1) with circular buffer or pointer
  - Peek: O(1)
  - Space: O(k) where k is the maximum queue capacity (fixed at 4 vehicles)

State Invariant:
  Only the vehicle at index 0 (Queue Position #1) is in the 'SERVING' state and
  eligible to receive fuel from the dispenser.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 LIFO STACK: TRANSACTION LEDGER & UNDO SYSTEM (TransactionStack)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Records every completed financial sale in Last-In, First-Out (LIFO) order,
  empowering the attendant to immediately undo/reverse the most recent sale if a
  mistake was made (e.g., wrong fuel dispensed or incorrect customer billing).

Real-World Analogy:
  A physical stack of paper receipts placed on an office receipt spindle.

Implementation Details:
  - Class: `TransactionStack`
  - Internal Structure: Array storage acting as a stack with push/pop operations.
  - Transaction Record Object:
    {
      id: "TX-5001",
      carId: "CAR-101",
      fuelType: "regular",
      fuelName: "Regular 87",
      litres: 24.5,
      pricePerLitre: 1.45,
      total: 35.53,
      timestamp: "15:42:10",
      satisfactionAtSale: 92
    }
  - Operations:
    * `push(transaction)`: Pushes a completed sale onto the top of the stack.
    * `pop()`: Removes and returns the most recent transaction to execute a refund.
    * `peek()`: Views the top of the stack without popping.
    * `isEmpty()`: Verifies if undo is currently possible.

Complexity:
  - Push: O(1)
  - Pop: O(1)
  - Peek: O(1)
  - Space: O(N) where N is total transactions completed during the shift.

Game Integration:
  Pressing [U] or clicking "Undo Last Sale" triggers `undoLastSale()`. This pops
  the transaction, refunds the money from `this.cash`, decrements `customersServed`,
  restocks the dispensed fuel volume back into `FuelHashMap`, and logs an undo entry.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 HASH MAP: FUEL INVENTORY & ORDER REGISTRY (FuelHashMap / CarOrderMap)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Provides instant O(1) key-value lookup, update, and retrieval for both fuel
  dispenser inventories and active customer orders.

1. Fuel Catalog & Inventory (`FuelHashMap`):
   - Key: String fuel identifier ('regular', 'premium', 'diesel').
   - Value:
     {
       name: "Regular 87",
       octane: 87,
       price: 1.45,
       stock: 350.0,
       capacity: 500.0,
       color: "#38bdf8"
     }
   - Operations:
     * `get(fuelType)`: Returns fuel specification in O(1) time.
     * `dispense(fuelType, amount)`: Deducts stock during pumping; handles out-of-stock.
     * `restock(fuelType, amount)`: Restores fuel tank levels via station terminal.

2. Car Order Registry (`CarOrderMap`):
   - Key: Unique vehicle string ID (e.g., "CAR-104").
   - Value: Complete customer order specification, target litres, tank capacity,
            and vehicle status.
   - Operations:
     * `setOrder(carId, orderData)`: O(1) registration on car arrival.
     * `getOrder(carId)`: O(1) instant lookup when car reaches the pump.
     * `removeOrder(carId)`: O(1) clean-up when car despawns past station exit.

Complexity:
  - Insert / Put: O(1) average
  - Lookup / Get: O(1) average
  - Delete / Remove: O(1) average
  - Space: O(M) where M is fuel types (3) + active world cars (<= 4).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 DYNAMIC ARRAY / LIST: DAILY SALES AUDIT LOG (DailySalesList)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Maintains an immutable chronological journal of all shift events, completed
  sales, and administrative refunds for financial auditing and reporting.

Implementation Details:
  - Class: `DailySalesList`
  - Operations:
    * `append(tx)`: Records confirmed sales chronologically.
    * `markRefunded(txId)`: Flags a sale as reversed after a Stack POP.
    * `computeTotalRevenue()`: Linear reduction of net active revenue.
    * `toArray()`: Exports data for report generation and terminal tables.

Complexity:
  - Append: O(1) amortized
  - Traversal: O(N)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 SINGLY LINKED LIST: SEQUENTIAL RECEIPT CHAIN & FINISH POINT CALCULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
  Maintains a dynamic sequential chain of vehicle receipts (`ReceiptNode` objects)
  where each node stores transaction payload and points to the next completed car (`.next`).
  At the shift Finish Point (10 cars completed), the attendant opens the Station
  Computer in the shop and executes an O(N) sequential traversal from HEAD to TAIL
  to compute and reconcile total shift revenue.

Implementation Details:
  - Class: `ReceiptLinkedList` (Nodes: `ReceiptNode`)
  - Operations:
    * `append(tx)`: O(1) tail insertion when a car payment is processed.
    * `removeById(txId)`: O(N) node unlinking if an undo reversal occurs.
    * `calculateTotal()`: O(N) sequential pointer traversal (`curr = curr.next`)
      accumulating total dollars and litres with step-by-step audit trace.
    * `toArray()`: Exports nodes for UI chain visualization in the terminal.

Complexity:
  - Append to Tail: O(1)
  - Sequential Traversal & Sum: O(N)
  - Space: O(N) where N is completed transactions.

4. CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANISMS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 VEHICLE ARRIVAL, QUEUEING & DEPARTURE PHYSICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Vehicles spawn along the entry road at Z = 28.0 (Lane X = -3.2).
- The FIFO Queue assigns each car a target slot coordinate:
    Slot 0 (Pump Island) : Z = 0.0
    Slot 1 (2nd in line) : Z = 7.5
    Slot 2 (3rd in line) : Z = 14.5
    Slot 3 (4th in line) : Z = 21.5
- Each frame, cars calculate the distance `distZ = position.z - targetZ`:
    * While moving, wheels rotate in sync with velocity: `wheel.rotation.x -= step * 1.8`.
    * A subtle sinusoidal chassis sway simulates suspension dynamics.
    * Upon arrival at Z = 0.0, the car transitions from 'ARRIVING' to 'SERVING'.
- Departing cars accelerate down the exit highway toward Z = -35.0, where they
  are automatically removed from the scene and cleaned up from `CarOrderMap`.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 FIRST-PERSON ATTENDANT CONTROLLER & MOUSE-LOOK RAYCASTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Camera System:
  * First-Person Mode (Default): Eye level at Y = 1.7m, 65° FOV, rotation order 'YXZ'.
  * Third-Person Mode [V]: Elevated station isometric view at (13, 11, 16).
- Pointer Lock & Mouse Look:
  * Clicking the game screen requests browser pointer lock.
  * Mouse movement calculates delta yaw and pitch with clamped vertical view:
      `fpPitch = clamp(fpPitch - deltaY * 0.0024, -1.38, 1.38)`
- Attendant First-Person Viewmodel:
  * When in first-person mode, the player character's third-person body parts are
    culled to avoid clipping.
  * A custom 3D viewmodel (right forearm, glove, dispenser nozzle, and rubber hose)
    is parented directly to the camera with walking bob and pumping recoil vibration.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 PRECISION AIM-TO-PUMP FUEL TARGET MECHANISM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To deliver an authentic, skill-based simulator experience rather than a generic
click-to-fill UI:
1. 3D Target Ring & Hitbox:
   - Built on the side of the car facing the pump island (local +X, world X = -2.25).
   - Composed of:
     * Glowing neon-teal RingGeometry (radius 0.20 to 0.32) that visibly pulses.
     * Invisible CircleGeometry (radius 0.36) with DoubleSide material for raycast tests.
2. Real-Time Raycasting:
   - Every frame, a Three.js Raycaster fires from the camera center (0, 0):
       `raycaster.setFromCamera(screenCenter, camera)`
   - Checks intersection against the active vehicle's `fuelCapHitMesh`.
3. Visual & Interactive Feedback:
   - Aim Missed: Ring remains neon teal; crosshair reticle is standard size.
   - Aim Hit: Ring glows bright white (0xffffff); HUD reticle expands with an
     animated pulsing teal aura (`.crosshair-reticle.fuel-cap-aimed`); HUD prompt reads:
     "✅ Nozzle on cap! Hold [Space] or [Left Click] to pump".
4. Strict Pumping Gating:
   - In first-person mode, fuel flow will NOT begin unless `isFuelCapAimed === true`.
   - If the player's aim drifts off the ring while pumping, fuel flow immediately stops
     and a warning toast prompts the player to re-align their aim.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 DYNAMIC 3D SPLINE RUBBER HOSE SIMULATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Constructed using a Three.js `CatmullRomCurve3` spline extruded into a `TubeGeometry`.
- Three dynamic anchor control points:
    Point 0 (Start) : Dispenser holster outlet (-0.65, 1.1, 0.0).
    Point 1 (Sag)   : Midpoint with gravitational sag (`mid.y = min(y0, y2) - 0.5`).
    Point 2 (End)   : Attendant's held nozzle OR vehicle fuel port anchor.
- Every frame, the tube geometry is dynamically updated, providing realistic,
  curving rubber hose physics as the attendant walks around the station.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 CUSTOMER PATIENCE, SATISFACTION & MISTAKE SCORING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Patience Degradation:
  * Every vehicle in the FIFO queue has a patience rating (100.0% to 0.0%).
  * Waiting cars decay at 0.9%/sec; the car at the pump decays at 0.4%/sec.
  * If patience hits 0.0%, the customer honks their horn in frustration, abandons
    the queue (FIFO walkout), and triggers a -12% station satisfaction penalty.
- Mistake Penalties:
  * Dispensing the wrong fuel type: Immediate -15% satisfaction penalty and
    increment of the mistakes counter.
  * Customer walkout: -12% satisfaction penalty.
- Reward:
  * Serving customers with >70% patience grants +3% satisfaction and faster tips.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.6 FUEL DISPENSING, STOCK DEPLETION & INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Continuous Dispensing:
  * Holding [Spacebar] or [Left Mouse Button] dispenses fuel at 6.0 Litres/sec.
  * Real-time LCD counters display Delivered Litres and Current Due ($).
  * Automatically shuts off when the exact requested volume is reached.
- Stock Depletion:
  * Each litre pumped reduces stock from `FuelHashMap`.
  * If stock reaches 0 L, the dispenser clicks off with an "Out of Stock" alarm.
  * The player can access the Station Computer Terminal [E] to order fuel tanker
    restocks using earned cash.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.7 LIFO TRANSACTION REVERSAL (UNDO) SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- When a customer payment is finalized:
    1. Transaction record is created.
    2. Pushed to `TransactionStack` (LIFO).
    3. Cash is added to the till.
- If the attendant immediately realizes an error:
    1. Press [U] or click "Undo [U]".
    2. The top transaction is popped from `TransactionStack`.
    3. Money is refunded (`this.cash -= saleCost`).
    4. The pumped fuel volume is returned to station storage in `FuelHashMap`.
    5. The sale in `DailySalesList` is flagged as 'REFUNDED'.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.8 PROCEDURAL AUDIO SYNTHESIS ENGINE (Web Audio API)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All sound effects are synthetically produced in real-time via `AudioContext`:
- Fuel Pumping: Band-pass filtered white noise creating liquid rushing sound.
- Car Engine Arrival: Low-frequency sawtooth oscillator ramping from 40Hz to 85Hz.
- Car Departure: Accelerating pitch envelope with engine rev harmonics.
- Car Horns: Dual square wave oscillators (440Hz + 554Hz) for realistic dissonance.
- Cash Chime: Dual high sine wave bells (1200Hz -> 1800Hz) with exponential decay.
- UI Feedback: Subtle resonant high-frequency clicks and warning buzzers.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.9 TAMIL VOICE INTERACTION & DIALOGUE SYSTEM (Web Speech API)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To provide cultural flavor and an immersive experience, the game includes full
Tamil voice interactions for both customers and the fuel attendant:
- Web Speech API Integration:
  * Selects native Tamil voices (`ta-IN` / `ta` e.g., Microsoft Valluvar, Google தமிழ்).
  * Automatically falls back to Indian regional phonetics if Tamil TTS is not
    installed in the host operating system.
- Floating Glassmorphic Speech Bubble:
  * Animated dialog bubble displayed at top-center of HUD during interactions.
  * Shows authentic Tamil script, phonetic English romanization, and English translation.
- Interactive Dialogue Events:
  * Car Arrival: Customers speak in Tamil requesting their desired fuel & litres
    (e.g., "வணக்கம்! ரெகுலர் பெட்ரோல் 25 லிட்டர் போடுங்க தம்பி!").
  * Nozzle Connect: Attendant confirms (e.g., "சரிங்க சார், மீட்டர் ஜீரோ பாத்துக்கோங்க!").
  * Full Tank: Attendant announces completion and total bill in Tamil.
  * Wrong Fuel: Customer shouts in Tamil ("யோவ்! என்ன தப்பான பெட்ரோல் போடுறீங்க?!").
  * Payment & Departure: Customer thanks attendant ("ரொம்ப நன்றி தம்பி! வரேன்!").
  * Impatient Walkout: Customer complains in Tamil before leaving.
  * Refund Undo: Attendant apologizes and confirms the refund.
- Controls & Toggles:
  * Toggle button in HUD navbar and keyboard shortcut [T] to toggle Tamil voice ON/OFF.

5. SHIFT LIFECYCLE & GAME LOOP
================================================================================

5.1 Shift Initiation:
  - Title Screen modal displays instructions, keybindings, and target shift goals
    (Serve 10 customers within 180 seconds).
  - Clicking "START SHIFT" initializes the Web Audio API, locks pointer controls,
    and spawns the first vehicle into the queue.

5.2 Active Shift Servicing Loop:
  1. Car arrives at entry road and is enqueued into `CustomerQueue` (FIFO).
  2. Car drives to Slot 0 (Pump Island), displays requested fuel type & volume.
  3. Attendant walks to pump, selects nozzle (Keys 1, 2, or 3).
  4. Attendant presses [E] to connect nozzle; rubber hose attaches to car.
  5. Attendant aims crosshair at the glowing fuel cap ring.
  6. Attendant holds [Space] or [Left Click] until tank reaches target litres.
  7. Attendant presses [E] to collect payment; receipt is pushed to `TransactionStack`.
  8. Serviced vehicle departs down highway; next vehicle in FIFO queue rolls up.

5.3 Shift Conclusion & DSA Performance Report:
  - Triggered when 10 customers are served OR the 180s timer reaches zero.
  - Generates comprehensive end-of-shift report card displaying:
    * Total Revenue Earned ($)
    * Customers Serviced vs Walkouts
    * Final Station Satisfaction Rating (%)
    * Mistakes Committed & Undone Sales
    * Detailed Data Structures Audit Log (Queue throughput, Stack pops, Hash Map lookups)
